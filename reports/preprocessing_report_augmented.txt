    The brief says choose one. Porter stemming mangles words into
    non-words, destroying the terms that carry the sentiment:

    word             PorterStemmer      WordNetLemmatizer 
    murdering        murder             murder            
    games            game               game              
    better           better             good              
    really           realli             really            
    terrible         terribl            terrible          
    communities      commun             community         
    was              wa                 be                
    studying         studi              study             

    'terrible' -> 'terribl' and 'really' -> 'realli' are the clearest
    losses: both are strong sentiment carriers, both survive lemmatization.
