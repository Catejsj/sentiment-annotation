[7] READING THESE NUMBERS
    Cohen's Kappa is agreement AFTER removing the agreement you would
    expect from two people guessing with the same label frequencies.
    That is why it is always lower than the raw percentage, and why it
    is the honest figure to quote.
      < 0.20 slight | 0.21-0.40 fair | 0.41-0.60 moderate
      0.61-0.80 substantial | > 0.80 almost perfect   (Landis & Koch 1977)
